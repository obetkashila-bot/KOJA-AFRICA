KOJA Research — Free Sources Layer

Additive patch based on the KOJA Research rollback/base app.

Adds free-source discovery alongside existing research providers:
- Web (DuckDuckGo)
- Wikipedia
- OpenAlex
- Crossref
- arXiv
- Open Library
- Internet Archive
- DOAB (when its public endpoint responds)
- KOJA Documents

Adds Free Sources and Books filters/tabs. Existing Google and other research functionality is preserved.
No SQL migration. No destructive changes.
